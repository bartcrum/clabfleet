      Welcome to clabfleet

      1. Pick or draw a lab. Open a topology below or in the sidebar, or start a new one from a template or a blank canvas.
      2. Deploy it. Deploy places the nodes on your hosts and wires them up; Activity shows containerlab's output.
      3. Work with it. Double-click a node for its CLI, see routing live, capture packets on a link.
